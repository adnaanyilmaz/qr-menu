## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, contemporary, and premium casual Turkish dining experience tailored for high-speed digital ordering and browsing. The visual tone bridges artisanal warmth with razor-sharp digital ergonomics. 

- **Personality:** Hospitable, appetizing, modern, confident, and meticulously organized.
- **Audience:** Smartphone-wielding diners, fast-casual patrons, and culinary enthusiasts seeking swift, sensory navigation across flame-roasted meats, freshly baked breads, and mezze.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Elevated Modern Fast-Casual. It combines a pure canvas with deliberate warm thermal accents, tactile ingredient tags, crisp borders, and mouth-watering culinary photography.
- **Experience Philosophy:** Mobile-first vertical velocity. High touch-target accessibility guarantees that browsing under diverse dining lighting (from bright patios to dim mood lighting) remains effortless and legible. On wider viewports, the interface locks into a disciplined, centered mobile canvas flanked by a clean, ambient architectural backdrop.

## Colors

The palette balances culinary passion with strict utilitarian legibility.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas is anchored to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure food photography pops with natural saturation and zero hue contamination.
  - Secondary card containers use an off-white tint (`#F9FAFB`) to create subtle contextual layering without dimming the clean aesthetic.
- **Brand Accents:**
  - **Primary (`#B91C1C`):** A deep Anatolian crimson red symbolizing flame-roasted cuts, sumac, and Turkish heritage. Used for dominant CTAs, active tab indicators, price highlights, and badge accents.
  - **Secondary & Tertiary (`#D97706` / `#EA580C`):** Warm amber and citrus fire tones used sparingly for spice ratings, chef recommendations, promotional bursts, and dietary tags (e.g., Halal, Fresh Grill).
- **Typography & Neutral Hierarchy:**
  - **Headings & Body (`#111827` / `#1F2937`):** Deep charcoal delivering maximum contrast against the pure white surface, preventing the optical fatigue caused by raw `#000000`.
  - **Muted & Metadata (`#4B5563` / `#6B7280`):** Charcoal tints reserved for preparation descriptions, portion notes, and inactive state icons.
  - **Structural Borders (`#E5E7EB`):** Crisp, feather-weight hairline dividers creating structural rhythm without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric, welcoming curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers, prices, and navigation labels with the utilitarian precision and rhythmic reading comfort of **Inter** for menu descriptions, allergens, and dietary metadata.

- **Headers:** Rendered in tight tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) with high font weights (`600` to `800`) to evoke a punchy, editorial food magazine character.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Menu pricing employs `price-tag` using Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular alignment features enabled (`tnum`) to keep price lists balanced and quickly scannable.
- **Description Body:** Inter is dialed to regular weights (`400`) at comfortable line heights (`1.4` to `1.5`) ensuring fast reading on low-end screens or under direct sunlight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for immediate one-handed thumb interaction on mobile devices, converting gracefully on larger screens into an elegant focused viewport.

- **Grid & Viewport Frame:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid view with `1rem` lateral page margins. Content occupies full screen width with a sticky bottom summary/cart bar and sticky top category pills.
  - **Tablet & Desktop (≥ 640px):** The menu container constrains to a centered mobile-mimicking canvas (maximum width `480px` or `540px`), surrounded by a subtle neutral framing canvas (`#F3F4F6`), providing desktop users a distraction-free, kiosk-like digital terminal experience.
- **Touch-Target Sizing:**
  - All interactive elements (increment counters, filter chips, cart buttons, ingredient modifiers) enforce a minimum touch bounding box of `48px x 48px`.
  - Item cards use comfortable vertical padding (`space-md`) to avoid accidental mis-taps when ordering in bustling environments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp hairline boundaries paired with soft, low-intensity warm ambient diffusion, preventing the interface from looking synthetic or heavy.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces for list items and menu body.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Pill Chips):** Subtle boundary defined by a `1px` solid border in `#E5E7EB` combined with an ultra-soft drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Filters, Sticky Headers & Modal Sheets):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`. Sticky bars have a light border (`#E5E7EB`) on their docking edge.
- **Level 3 (Floating Order Action Bar / Floating Basket):** Lifted with high tactile confidence: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08)`. The slight primary crimson tint in the ambient shadow lends a signature brand glow.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (level 2) to convey approachable warmth while preserving structure:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to food card thumbnails, input fields, and standard modifier selector buttons.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to category banners, promo cards, and interactive detail bottom-sheets.
- **Full Pill Radius (9999px):** Applied exclusively to category navigation chips, add/counter controls, spicy/dietary indicator badges, and main call-to-action buttons. This distinction cleanly separates static cards from clickable, interactive controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Counters
- **Primary CTA:** Deep crimson background (`#B91C1C`), crisp white text, bold font (`label-lg`), pill-shaped. On press: subtle scale down (`0.98`) with active state (`#991B1B`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E5E7EB`, text `#1F2937`.
- **Quantity Step Counter:** Pill capsule with light neutral fill (`#F3F4F6`), containing deep crimson plus/minus icon controls (`40px` tap area minimum) flanking bold center numbers.

### Navigation Chips (Category Scroller)
- Horizontal scroll container with hidden scrollbars and edge fade.
- **Active Chip:** Crimson fill (`#B91C1C`), white text, bold weight.
- **Inactive Chip:** Pure white background, `1px` border `#E5E7EB`, text `#4B5563`. Smooth color and border transitions on touch.

### Menu Item Cards
- **Compact List Variant:** Left-aligned text content (dish title, price, description limited to 2 lines), paired with a right-aligned square image (`88px x 88px` or `96px x 96px`, `rounded-lg`). Quick-add `+` button positioned at the bottom right corner with an elevated touch surface.
- **Hero / Featured Variant:** Top full-width photographic showcase with ratio `16:9`, overlaid with dietary chips (e.g., "Special Grill", "Halal") in the top corner. Bottom segment houses item title, detailed spice notes, price, and full-width customizable add button.

### Badges & Dietary Tags
- Compact pill-shaped tags (`label-sm`).
- **Spicy / Chef Special:** Amber-orange soft fill (`#FEF3C7`), text `#D97706` or crimson tint (`#FEE2E2` with text `#B91C1C`).
- **Vegetarian / Vegan:** Soft emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), text `#047857`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Rounded (`rounded-md`), `48px` height, `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E5E7EB` border.
- Active focus displays a `2px` ring in brand crimson (`#B91C1C`) with zero offset. Left icon slots feature search loupe or cutlery icons in `#9CA3AF`.

### Sticky Bottom Order Tray
- Docked flush to viewport base with `safe-area-inset-bottom` support.
- Pure white surface, subtle top border (`#E5E7EB`), ambient shadow level 3. Features item count badge, live running total in high-contrast charcoal, and a crimson checkout trigger button.
